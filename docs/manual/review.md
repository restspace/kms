# Review

**Sidebar:** Review

This is the reviewer's own workspace — where scoring actually happens once an
[evaluation plan](evaluation.md) has assigned submissions to them. Reviewers see only this
screen; organisers and admins can also open it to see what a reviewer sees.

## The queue

A panel on the left lists every assignment as a button: code, plan name, submission title, and a
status chip (reviewed / in progress / complete / skipped). A "closed" chip appears if that plan's
review window has already ended. There's no separate filter dropdown — the status chips are how
you tell what's left to do. Clicking an item loads it into the scoring form on the right.

The header above the queue shows your progress as "N of M reviewed."

## Scoring a submission

- Each criterion from the plan renders as the right kind of control: number buttons for a scale
  criterion (showing its weight), a dropdown for a choice criterion, or a text box for an
  optional long-text criterion.
- **Your rationale** — a free-text box for your overall comment. Once every reviewer on this
  submission has submitted, this becomes visible to your co-reviewers as part of a shared
  discussion (see below).
- **Flag conflict of interest (skips scoring)** — a checkbox that disables every score input and
  lets you save without answering any criteria, for a submission you shouldn't be rating.
- **Save & Next** saves your review and automatically advances to the next unreviewed (or
  unskipped) item in your queue. **Save** just saves. Both are disabled until every scoreable
  criterion has an answer (or the conflict-of-interest box is checked), and while the plan's
  review window is closed.

  > **Note:** there's no autosave here and no keyboard shortcuts — every save is a deliberate
  > click.

## Discussion

Once you've submitted your own review, a discussion panel unlocks underneath the scoring form —
a running, append-only comment thread with your fellow reviewers on that submission. Before you've
submitted, it stays locked with a prompt to post your review first, so nobody's score is
influenced by seeing others' opinions ahead of time.
