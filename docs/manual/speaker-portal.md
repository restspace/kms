# The speaker portal

The portal is where speakers manage their own information after they've submitted — no separate
account creation, no password. This page covers both what a speaker sees, and how you configure
what they're asked to do.

## For speakers: getting in

Click the link in your submission confirmation email, or the "Continue to portal" button after
submitting, or go to your event's portal address and enter your email — you'll get a sign-in link
by email, valid for one use. You stay signed in for a week without needing to sign in again.

Once in, four sections sit across the top: **Home · Submissions · Profile · Tasks**.

- **Home** — your submissions at a glance with their current status (Accepted, Pending, Declined,
  Draft, Withdrawn), a summary of your profile, and your outstanding tasks.
- **Submissions** — full detail on everything you've submitted. You can still **edit** a
  submission after submitting it — even after it's been accepted — right up until it's declined
  or you withdraw it. Once organisers have started deciding on it, a couple of answers may show
  with a padlock: those are the ones that determined which review track the proposal went to, and
  they can't move at that point. Everything else stays editable. You can also **withdraw** a
  submission yourself if your plans change.
- **Profile** — your bio, headshot, pronouns, company/title, and links (LinkedIn, X, website).
  This is the one place you update your own information — organisers don't edit it for you.
  A short biography and a headshot are what make your listing complete on the organiser's side,
  so it's worth filling both in even before you're asked.
- **Tasks** — anything the organiser needs from you: uploading your slides, confirming details,
  filling in a short form, or visiting an external link. Each task shows what's needed and its due
  date; overdue ones are marked clearly. Completing one is straightforward — drop a file, tick a
  box, or fill in the form, whichever the task calls for.

## For organisers: setting up what speakers see

Tasks are created and assigned from [Workspace → Tasks](workspace-tasks.md), not a separate
"Portals" area — see that page for the create form's fields (target, assignment mode, trigger,
action type, due date and reminder offsets). The task's **action type** determines what a speaker
actually does with it: upload a file, fill in a form, acknowledge something (tick a box to confirm
they've read it), or visit an external link and confirm they've done so.

## Tracking who owes you what

Rather than chasing speakers one by one, use the [Speaker Tracking](dashboard.md#speaker-tracking)
dashboard — it lists everyone with an outstanding task, sorted by how many they're behind on, plus
a one-click **Send reminder** (or **Remind all outstanding**) for anything overdue.
